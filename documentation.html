1. Executive Summary & Problem-Solution Fit

1.1 Real-World Pakistani Food Ordering Bottlenecks

The quick-service restaurant (QSR) and fast-food industry in Pakistan experiences severe operational challenges during lunch and dinner rushes that standard Western software suites cannot solve:

  • Extreme Peak-Hour Telephone Congestion: Between 8:00 PM and 11:30 PM, restaurant phone lines are continuously engaged. Customers face 5–10 minute hold times, causing a 30–40% abandonment rate.
  • Colloquial Language & Slang (Code-Switching): Customers speak in a seamless blend of Roman Urdu and English (e.g., "Bhai 2 zinger burger aur aik thanda pepsi bhej do", "nahi 3 nahi 2 kar do", "extra spicy sauce daalna"). Traditional English voicebots fail to parse these phonetic nuances.
  • Cash-on-Delivery (COD) Refusal & Fake Order Losses: Over 85% of restaurant deliveries in Pakistan are settled in cash at the doorstep. Restaurants suffer substantial food waste and dispatch costs when customers refuse delivery or submit fake orders.
  • Kitchen Miscommunication & Lost Tickets: Telephone order-takers write paper slips by hand or manually enter items into the POS, leading to missing modifiers, incorrect quantities, and delayed dispatches.
  • Selling 86'd (Out-of-Stock) Dishes: When key kitchen inventory runs out during a rush, telephone agents continue taking orders for those dishes due to a lack of real-time communication.
  • Customer Frustration & Churn: Conventional bots get trapped in repetitive loops without providing a way to reach a human manager.

1.2 How Dastarkhwan AI Automates the Operations

Traditional Problem Dastarkhwan AI Automated Solution
Busy Phone Lines & Hold Times 0ms Automated Concurrency: AI Voice and Chat agents process multiple orders simultaneously 24/7 without hold times.
Roman Urdu & Slang Misunderstandings Custom Keyword & Entity Parser: Grounds customer intent against menu aliases and recognizes Roman Urdu numerals (do, teen, ek, char), Urdu script (ایک, دو), and English words.
Wrong Items & Customer Misunderstandings Automatic Bill Read-Back: The AI summarizes the itemized bill, delivery charges, and address, requiring explicit confirmation ("Haan" / "Ji sahi hai") before cooking starts.
Fake Orders & COD Fraud Explainable Heuristic Risk Scorer: Automatically evaluates order risk (0–100) based on past cancellations, basket value, and customer history. High-risk orders trigger an automated verification phone call.
Delayed Kitchen Punching Instant Live Kanban Dispatch: Confirmed orders appear instantly in the kitchen's "New" column with a Web Audio API two-tone bell chime and visual highlight animation.
Selling Sold-Out (86'd) Items Real-Time 86'd Stock Sync: Toggling an item to "Off" in the admin dashboard instantly informs all voice and chat agents to reject that item and suggest available alternatives.
Angry Customer Churn Sentiment Analysis & Human Handoff: Detects negative sentiment keywords (bakwas, ghussa, fuzool) or repeated misunderstandings and routes the conversation to a Store Manager console.

2.7 Page 4: Human Handoff & Attention Desk (/handoff)

  • Customer Escalation Queue: Lists all customer conversations requiring human intervention with customer name, phone number, and timestamp.
  • Escalation Triggers:
    • Customer Frustration / Anger: Triggered by sentiment analysis (bakwas, ghussa, fuzool, fraud).
    • Manager Request: Triggered by phrases like talk to human or manager se baat karni hai.
    • Double Comprehension Failure: Triggered when the AI fails to parse intent twice consecutively.
  • Manager Takeover Console: Displays the full chat transcript and allows the store manager to send messages with an official [Store Manager] tag.
  • "Resolve & Return to AI" Button: Closes the escalation and removes it from the attention queue.
  • "Simulate Escalation" Button: Test button to simulate a customer escalation on demand.

2.8 Page 5: Fake-Order Risk & Verification (/risk)

  • Risk Filter Buttons: Filters orders by All, High Risk, Medium, and Low.
  • Explainable Fraud Heuristic Rules:
    • Past Delivery Refusals: 3+ cancellations adds +45 risk points; 1–2 adds +25 points.
    • First-Time Customer: Unverified address adds +15 points.
    • High COD Basket Value: Orders over Rs. 3,000 add +15 points; over Rs. 5,000 add +30 points.
    • Suspicious Item Quantities: Single item qty ≥ 4 or total items ≥ 7 adds +15 points.
  • Order Verification Card: Displays numerical risk score bar (0–100), itemized breakdown reasons, and action buttons (Call Customer, Verify Order, Reject & Cancel).

  • Best Sellers Leaderboard: Top 5 revenue-generating dishes with order counts, PKR revenues, and progress bars.
  • Channel Breakdown: Volume split between Voice Agent, Chat Agent, and Web Menu.
